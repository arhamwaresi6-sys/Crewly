✦ FAQ

Quick answers

Can other people change my card?

No. Everyone can view every card in the event, but only the card's owner can change it.

Who can change the total expense?

The event owner (the person who created it). The equal split recalculates automatically.

How do I invite someone?

While creating an event, type their Gmail. If they are registered, they show up in the list – tap to invite.

Is the money moved through the app?

No. SplitWise+ only tracks who owes what. You settle outside the app.
